Safeguarding and data

How the full version protects boys. Items marked “in this demo” already work in this prototype.

Sign-in

Boys and staff use their existing Microsoft 365 school accounts. Years 4 and 5 can use a printed QR code card with a PIN of at least eight digits, so there is no password to remember. Codes and PINs are never emailed to boys.

Photos

Boys photograph the thing, not themselves. Location, camera and time data are stripped when a photo is saved, and it is re-saved at a smaller size. Faces never appear on a share page. In this demo

Share links

Off until a boy makes one. First name only, a random 128-bit code, an expiry date, one-click switch-off and a record of each visit. Search engines are told not to list the page. In this demo

Who sees what

Staff see only the boys in their scope: a Division Master sees their division, activity staff see only the challenges assigned to them. Parents see signed-off entries, and boys are told this. There are no leaderboards and boys never see each other's progress. In this demo

Concerns

Every logging screen has a “Worried about something?” route. The DSL can see all records, including anything flagged, and can switch off any link. Staff cannot edit a boy's reflections. In this demo

Retention

Proposed: evidence kept while a boy is at school; keepsake produced in his final term; evidence and photos deleted 12 months after he leaves; share links stop when he leaves. To be aligned with the school's Retention Policy.

Compliance before a pilot

DPIACompleted and signed off before any real data is used. This prototype uses fictitious data only.
UK GDPR and the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025Main provisions in force from 5 February 2026; complaints procedure from 19 June 2026. The app is covered by the school's complaints procedure.
KCSIE 2026In force from 1 September 2026. The app joins the annual filtering, monitoring and cyber security review.
ICO Children's CodeNot binding on schools, but used as the standard: high-privacy defaults, no nudges, no profiling, geolocation off.
HostingAzure UK South, with evidence in the app's own storage so metadata stripping, expiring links and scheduled deletion are under the school's control.
